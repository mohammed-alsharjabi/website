## About Me

I build scalable, product-focused mobile applications with Flutter and turn complex business requirements into maintainable engineering systems. My work combines mobile architecture, API integration, Arabic-first UX, performance, and practical product thinking across applications, dashboards, commerce, logistics, education, and service platforms.

As the founder of **TechnoVizen**, I lead technical delivery, mentor developers, and create Arabic technical content that connects software engineering with real business outcomes.

- Keep business rules independent from frameworks and UI.
- Treat Arabic RTL as a first-class product experience.
- Make loading, error, empty, offline, and permission states part of the design.
- Measure engineering success through product reliability and user outcomes.
